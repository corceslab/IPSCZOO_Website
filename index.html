The iPSC Zoo is a reference collection of iPSC-derived cell types, contributed by many laboratories using their own differentiation protocols and profiled under a single pipeline in the Corces Lab. This site reports bulk RNA-seq expression for any gene across those cell types, protocols, iPSC lines and labs.

It does not recommend a protocol. Expression is one of several considerations, alongside the iPSC line, the number of samples behind a value, and data this site does not hold.

Methods

Expression unit
TPM (transcripts per million). TPM corrects for gene length and sequencing depth, so a gene is comparable across samples. It is a within-sample proportion, so values are not directly comparable between genes, and they are not raw counts.
Processing
nf-core/rnaseq (STAR alignment, Salmon quantification), run 260213_full_run. Values are reported as produced by the pipeline; this site applies no further normalisation.
Gene annotation
GENCODE protein-coding genes. Non-coding biotypes are excluded. Where several annotation rows share a gene symbol, their values are summed.
Sample annotation
RNA-seq samples. Cell type, protocol, lab, iPSC line and batch are parsed from each sample name and cross-checked against the Zoo sample sheet.
Protocol key
Protocol names, types and references are taken from the Zoo protocol annotations; most protocols link to their source publication on PubMed from the Protocols tab.

Reading the data

Several genes
The comparison grid shades each row against its own maximum, because TPM compares a gene across samples rather than one gene against another. Figures shown are means.
By protocol
One bar per protocol within a cell type, in numerical order. Protocols are never ordered by expression.
Log scale
log10(TPM + 1).
Cell-type labels
exNiAst and inNiAst are NFIB/SOX9-induced astrocyte variants (excitatory and inhibitory co-culture, labelled exc/inh in the sample sheet) and are shown beside the other astrocytes.
Control
K562 is an immortalised control cell line included in every batch, shown in grey and excluded from the brain-cell view.
Identifiers
Labs appear as codes. Line “not reported” means no line was recorded for that sample.

Limitations

  • Many protocol and line combinations are represented by a single sample. Sample counts are shown beside every value and should be read with it.
  • Expression differs between iPSC lines on the same protocol, so a protocol-level mean can conceal line-to-line variation.
  • Bulk RNA-seq reports the average of a culture. High expression of a marker does not establish purity, and low expression does not exclude a minority population.
  • Sample metadata is parsed from sample names; discrepancies between names and the Zoo sample sheet are reconciled with the Zoo team as they are found.
  • These data are unpublished. Figures may change as the collection grows and processing is revised.
